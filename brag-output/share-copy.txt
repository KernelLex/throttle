Built Throttle: an email scheduler that shows you the delivery forecast before you
send anything. The chart isn't an estimate of the schedule — the same planSchedule()
function draws it in the browser and writes it on the server.

1,000 emails across 3 senders at 50/hour: 7 hour windows, finishes in 6h 40m. Hit the
rate limit and the surplus rolls into the next window in order. Kill the process
mid-campaign and the schedule survives.

Nothing dropped. Nothing sent twice. throttle-delta.vercel.app
