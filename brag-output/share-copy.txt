Built Throttle — an email scheduler that shows you the delivery forecast before you
send anything. The chart isn't an estimate of the schedule: the same planSchedule()
function draws it in the browser and writes it on the server.

1,000 emails across 3 senders at 50/hour → 7 hour windows, finishing in 6h 40m. Hit
the hourly limit and the surplus rolls into the next window in order, with one Slack
alert per sender per window. Fail a sender five times and its circuit opens, traffic
reroutes by health score, and it probes its way back. Kill the worker mid-campaign and
the schedule survives — Postgres is the source of truth, the reconciler rebuilds Redis
on boot.

No cron anywhere, not even BullMQ's repeat:. Nothing dropped. Nothing sent twice.
